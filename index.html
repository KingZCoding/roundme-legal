Meet the people around you.

RoundMe shows you who is nearby and lets you meet them on your terms. Browse locals, play the swipe game, post to your area and chat when it clicks.

Coming soon to Google Play Android first. For people 18 and older.

What you can do

Everything is built around who is actually near you.

Locals

See who is nearby, closest first, with profiles that show only what each person chooses to share.

Meet

Swipe through people, match when the interest is mutual, and play the guessing game to break the ice.

Live Feed

Post to the people around you. Reply and like. Posts stay up for 7 days, so the feed always feels fresh.

Chat

Message your matches and friends. You decide who is allowed to start a conversation with you.

Friends

Add the people you click with, and keep them close even when you are not in the same place.

Built to keep you in control

Meeting strangers should feel safe. These are not extras, they are how RoundMe works.

  • Your exact location stays private. Other people see your town and a rough distance, never where you are.
  • You choose what your profile shows. Hidden details are not sent to anyone else.
  • Block and report in a tap. Blocked people cannot see or contact you.
  • Adults only. You confirm your birthday when you sign up, and RoundMe is for people 18 and older.
  • No ads and no selling your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
